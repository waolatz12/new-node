1. INTRODUCTION
This report serves as a summary of my tenure as the Choir Secretary of LFC Idi Aba, Abeokuta, spanning March 2023 to March 2025. It highlights key responsibilities, achievements, challenges encountered, solutions implemented, and recommendations for the incoming secretary.
2. CHOIR MEMBERSHIP AND ATTENDANCE OVERVIEW
•	Choir Membership Statistics:
o	Membership at the beginning of tenure (April 2022): 64 members
o	New members joined during tenure: 17 members (4 left)
o	Members who left during tenure: 18 members 
o	Total membership as of March 2025: 62 members
o	Total newborn babies: 10 babies
o	Total dedicated cars: 3 cars
o	Total persons who relocated (locally): 11 members
o	Total person who relocated (internationally): 3 members
o	Total weddings: 3 weddings
•	Attendance Trends:
o	Average attendance at weekly rehearsals: 40 members
o	Average attendance at Sunday services:  38 members
Challenges Faced:
1.	Inconsistent Attendance: Some members were irregular in rehearsals and Sunday services.
2.	Lack of Timely Information from Some Members: Members failed to inform leadership about absences or resignations.
3. REPORTS TO THE CHURCH
As part of my responsibilities, I submitted monthly reports to the church leadership, although I missed some months due to forgetfulness and unforeseen discrepancies. These reports included:
•	Choir Population Report: Membership updates, attendance trends, and retention rate.
•	Event Participation Report: Choir involvement in church services, special programs, and external ministrations.
•	Challenges Report: Issues encountered and resolutions adopted.
•	General Choir Progress Report: Summary of activities, growth, and notable achievements.
4. COORDINATION OF EXECUTIVE MEETINGS
•	Total Meetings Held: Around 12 executive meetings over two years.
•	Meeting Outcomes:
o	Decision-making on choir administration and welfare.
o	Planning and execution of major choir events.
o	Review of disciplinary measures where necessary.
o	Conflict resolution among choir members.
•	Challenges:
o	Difficulty in ensuring full attendance of executives.
o	Some decisions took longer than expected to implement..
5. ONLINE AND INTERNAL COMMUNICATION
•	Managed the official choir WhatsApp platform.
•	Sent timely reminders about rehearsals and church programs.
•	Shared updates from church leadership with choir members.
•	Added new members to the platform and removed inactive members.
•	Challenges:
o	Some members ignored important messages.
o	Difficulty in reaching members who were not active online.
•	Solution: Used a combination of group messages and direct calls for critical updates.
6. MAJOR EVENTS AND PARTICIPATION
During my tenure, the choir also acquired and improved several instruments and equipment to enhance our performances:
•	New Equipment Acquired: Hanging microphones, gangan (talking drum), complete row of wire, drum pedals, hangers, extension box, and curtains for the choir robe.
•	Repairs and Other items bought: 
o	Fixed the choir store
o	Fixed the bass drum vellum multiple times
o	Purchased choir wardrobe and plastic containers (for ties and roses)
o	Repaired the keyboard.
During my tenure, the choir actively participated in several church programs, including:
•	Easter and Christmas Services: Special ministrations were rendered.
•	Leadership Summit Worship Sessions: The choir ministered during key sessions.
•	Outreach and Evangelism Programs: Choir members participated in gospel outreaches.
The choir also hosted programs (some among the choir members, others open to the church):
•	Christmas Carol Programmes: ministered in songs, provided feeding to both church and the choir members, and invited guests.
•	Love Feast: The event was indoor among the choir members and solely for the purpose of bonding and worshipping together as a group.
•	Retreats: This was mainly to edify and strengthen each other, praying for one another, and for the church.


7. CHALLENGES FACED AND SOLUTIONS IMPLEMENTED
Challenges:
1.	Poor Commitment from Some Members: Some members were passive and only participated occasionally.
2.	Communication Gaps: Not all members were responsive to information shared online.
3.	Event Planning Difficulties: Short notice for special ministrations affected preparedness.
Solutions:
•	Strengthened mentorship and follow-up for new and inactive members.
•	Encouraged leaders to have one-on-one check-ins with members.
•	Established an event calendar for better planning.
8. RECOMMENDATIONS FOR THE INCOMING SECRETARY
1.	Improve Attendance Monitoring: Implement a digital attendance system for better tracking.
2.	Enhance Communication: Encourage the use of multiple channels (WhatsApp, calls, physical meetings) to reach all members.
3.	Streamline Reporting to the Church: Keep detailed records and use a template for efficiency.
4.	Increase Engagement: Organize team-building activities to foster unity and commitment.
5.	Enhance Documentation: Keep a structured file of meeting minutes, reports, and event plans for easy handover.
9. CONCLUSION
It has been an honor serving as the Choir Secretary for the past two years. I am grateful for the support from the choir coordinator, assistant, executives, and choir members. I pray for continued growth and excellence in the choir. May God bless the incoming leadership with wisdom and strength to take the choir to greater heights.
